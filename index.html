个人简介 About

朱鹏程，哈尔滨工业大学电气工程专业博士研究生（2022年入学，预计2027年3月毕业）， 师从国家高层次人才、特聘教授徐永向老师。2025年9月起于西班牙塞维利亚大学进行联合培养， 合作导师为 IEEE Fellow、IEEE Trans. Ind. Electron. 联合主编 Sergio Vazquez 教授。 主要研究方向为基于单直流母线电流传感器的永磁同步电机（PMSM）驱动控制与模型预测控制。 以第一作者（含导师一作、本人二作）在 IEEE Trans. Ind. Electron.、IEEE JESTPE、 IEEE Trans. Instrum. Meas.、中国电机工程学报等高水平期刊发表多篇论文。 担任 IEEE Trans. Power Electron.、IEEE Trans. Ind. Electron. 审稿人。获中国国际大学生创新大赛国家银奖（团队负责人）、 黑龙江省三好学生、哈工大优秀学生等荣誉。多次深入参与国防及工业现场调试，具备扎实的工程实践能力。

Pengcheng Zhu is a Ph.D. candidate in Electrical Engineering at Harbin Institute of Technology (2022–Present, expected March 2027), under the supervision of Prof. Yongxiang Xu (National High-Level Talent, Distinguished Professor). Since September 2025, he has been a joint Ph.D. student at the University of Seville, Spain, working with Prof. Sergio Vazquez (IEEE Fellow, Co-Editor-in-Chief of IEEE Trans. Ind. Electron.). His research focuses on single DC-link current sensor control and model predictive control for PMSM drives. He has published multiple papers as first author in top-tier journals including IEEE Trans. Ind. Electron., IEEE JESTPE, IEEE Trans. Instrum. Meas., and Proceedings of the CSEE. He serves as a reviewer for IEEE Trans. Power Electron. and IEEE Trans. Ind. Electron. He has received numerous honors including the National Silver Award at CICSIC 2024 (Team Leader). He has extensive hands-on experience in defense and industrial field commissioning.
